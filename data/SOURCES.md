# Data sources

Snapshot collected on **2026-11-11**. Every request respected `robots.txt`, waited at least 2.5 seconds,
used no login, and collected no personal data and no review text.

Clean tables built from these files by `src/build_public.py` are in `data/public/` and in `dbt/seeds/public/`.

### Sources tried and skipped
- **Trustpilot**: its `robots.txt` disallows all generic crawlers (`User-agent: * Disallow: /`). Not collected; on-site ratings used instead.
- **Meta Ad Library / Google Ads Transparency Center**: need a login or automated browsing of pages not meant for it. Not collected.
- **Lithuanian register (headcount, average salary)**: no unambiguous public match to the right legal entity. Not used.
